# Wellness

> A private space to reflect, express gratitude, and let go of negative energy.

---

# Overview

**Wellness** is a simple and supportive mental health journaling application that helps users organize their thoughts, practice gratitude, and release negative emotions through structured daily journaling.

The application provides a safe, customizable, and private environment where users can reflect on their day, acknowledge positive experiences, and process difficult emotions.

---

# Purpose

Wellness helps users:

* Record daily thoughts and experiences
* Develop a gratitude habit
* Process and release negative emotions
* Build self-awareness through reflection
* Create a sustainable journaling routine
* Track emotional well-being over time
* Customize their journaling environment for comfort and accessibility

---

# Core Features

## 1. Daily Journal

The primary journaling area where users can freely write about their thoughts, emotions, experiences, goals, or anything on their mind.

### Fields

* Journal Title (optional)
* Date and Time
* Journal Entry

### Example Prompt

> "How are you feeling today?"

---

## 2. Gratitude Section

A dedicated section that encourages users to focus on positive aspects of their lives.

### Purpose

Help users recognize and appreciate anything they are thankful for without limiting the number of entries.

### Features

* Dynamic gratitude list
* Add gratitude item
* Edit gratitude item
* Remove gratitude item
* Unlimited gratitude entries

### Example Prompt

> "What are you grateful for today?"

---

## 3. Negative Energy Release

A dedicated space where users can express frustrations, worries, fears, stress, or negative thoughts without judgment.

### Purpose

Provide a healthy emotional outlet and help users process difficult feelings.

### Fields

* Negative Thoughts
* Concerns
* Stressors
* Things I Want to Let Go Of

### Example Prompt

> "What thoughts or feelings would you like to release today?"

---

# Journal Entry Structure

```text
Date: June 4, 2026

DAILY JOURNAL
Today I felt...

GRATITUDE
- I am grateful for...
- I am grateful for...
- Continue adding as needed

NEGATIVE ENERGY RELEASE
- I am worried about...
- I feel frustrated because...
- I want to let go of...
```
